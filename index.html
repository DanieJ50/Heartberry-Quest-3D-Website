THE CHARACTER ABILITY LOOP

Four ideas. One system.

Instead of choosing one mechanic, Heartberry Quest uses each at a different point in the player journey.

01MEET

One Berry per tutorial

A region introduces its hero and teaches that unique power.

02UNLOCK

Finish their chapter

Completing a Berry’s introduction adds them to your helper roster.

03CHOOSE

Pick a helper

Before regular levels, choose the Berry whose strategy best fits the board.

04CHARGE

Match and activate

Matches fill the meter until the chosen Berry’s power is ready.
